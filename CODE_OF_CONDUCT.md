# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a small tool maintained in spare time. Everyone is welcome to use it,
report problems and send patches — regardless of experience level, language, or
background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the code, kind about the person.
- Assume good faith: most reports come from someone who hit a real problem.
- Keep issues on topic. Bugs in Discord itself, or in a VPN provider, are out of scope.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no: the project deliberately stays small and
  dependency-free.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any project space.
- Publishing someone's private data — including tokens, nicknames, paths or screenshots
  with personal information.
- Using the issue tracker to ask for help with account theft, ban evasion, or anything
  aimed at an account you do not own.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where the project
is represented.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer via <https://github.com/Apatiym>. Reports are
handled confidentially. Depending on severity, the response is a warning, a deleted
comment, or a block from the repository.
